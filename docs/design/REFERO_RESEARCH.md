# Refero Styles research

Research date: 2026-07-18

Scope: DESIGN-00 / TEMPLATE-LAB-00
Source count: 42 distinct public style pages

## Method and limits

This study used the public Refero Styles index and public style pages. Discovery combined the
requested keywords — photography, portfolio, editorial, gallery, brutalist, typography,
minimal, darkroom, cinematic, art direction, fashion, magazine, monochrome, swiss,
experimental, luxury, poster, immersive, grid, and fullscreen — with direct page checks.

Only abstract design principles were recorded. No account, MCP integration, cookie, access
control bypass, screenshot, image, font, logo, downloadable DESIGN.md, or long third-party
passage was used. Source names below identify citations only; they are not candidate IDs and
must not appear in the Template Lab registry. Exact brand tokens, signature compositions,
copy, imagery, and typefaces are explicitly excluded from implementation.

The overlap notes compare each source with the eleven formal FRAME//ZERO templates frozen in
`app/templates/catalog.ts`. A high overlap is useful evidence for rejecting a candidate, not a
reason to imitate the source more closely.

- Do not copy: Exact colors, geometric illustration, service copy, brand name, font, or pills.

## Cross-source principles worth translating

- Let photography own chromatic richness; keep interface color sparse and role-based.
- Separate full-body, portrait, detail, and panorama frames through scale and placement rather
  than decorative effects.
- Keep default copy outside the central portrait focus zone and offer edge captions or dedicated
  metadata bands.
- Alternate dense indexes with quiet focus moments so discovery and immersion do not compete.
- Make every non-standard scroll area visibly focusable, labelled, and usable without a pointer.
- Use section polarity, grid rules, spacing, and type scale before adding shadows or glass.
- Treat reduced motion as a complete reading mode, not merely a slower animation setting.
- On small screens, preserve the information order even when spatial composition collapses.

## Patterns deliberately rejected

- Brand logos, wordmarks, slogans, proprietary faces, exact token sets, and signature object motifs.
- Screenshot collages, downloaded imagery, remote fonts, and media whose reuse rights are unknown.
- WebGL, canvas scenes, particle fields, long decorative loaders, and every-element motion.
- Direct replicas of fashion commerce, streaming, travel booking, agency case-study, or product
  catalogue interactions.
- Candidate identities derived from any source or company name.
